About Me

Hello, my name is Noah Platts!

I am a Product Design student at Drexel University with a minor in User Experience User Design.

My Design Philosophy

I wanted to design with emphasis on longevity and modularity with a hope to reduce one year life cycle products and to promote repair and reuse.

Why I Want to Pursue Design

I have always been fascinated by exploration and engineering since childhood. Playing with Legos, Erector Set, or just wooden blocks, alongside having parents who were both in the creative and arts field, as well as taking art classes through my entire academic career. I first became aware of this industry in design from taking high school shop and CAD classes. I remember having a 7:00 AM class remotely, when everything was shut down due to Covid-19, and the first project was finding a solution to a bakery needing delivery boxes that could safely transport various sizes and weights of pastries. I loved the whole process, from brainstorming to rough cardboard prototypes, to eventually 3D modeling and animating how it functions.
